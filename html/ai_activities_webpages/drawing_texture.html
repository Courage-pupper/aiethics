Drawing plus Textures

In this activity, you will create artwork using art materials and use AI to combine patterns and textures you make yourself. Using crafting materials, you will generate textures that can transform your artwork. By experimenting with how real-world textures change your digital image, you will engage with ethics principles. You can do this alone, with a partner, or in a small group.

What's Needed

Also use other materials you can think of.

  • Texture materials, for example:
    • Fabric
    • Cotton
    • Toothpicks
    • Paperclips
  • Cardstock
  • Pens and colored pencils

Guiding Questions for AI Ethics Literacy

Based on OECD AI principles.

Creativity and human capabilities
  • Creativity: Did the AI mixing enhance your creativity?
  • Human capabilities: Was the AI a helpful tool that augmented your abilities, or did it feel like it took over the creative process?
  • Representation: Does technology like this make artistic expression more inclusive and accessible to people who may not have art training?
Fairness and agency
  • Agency: In this activity, you created a drawing and a texture — who is the primary author of the final image?
Transparency and explainability
  • AI outcomes: Can you trace a line and explain how the AI decided to apply the texture to specific parts of your drawing?
  • Understanding AI: What could the AI do to help you understand its outcomes better?
Robustness and safety
  • Robustness: Did the AI create a good result, or did it produce errors or strange artifacts?
  • Speculating use: What are some uses for a technology that can apply the texture of one thing to the shape/drawing of another?

Activity Instructions

1

Create an artwork

Use art materials to draw something you like. Use any of the materials available to you. Take a picture once you are done.

2

Create a texture

Use a maximum of two materials to create a pattern or texture, such as fabrics, pins, or pieces of paper. Take a picture of your pattern.

3

Combine with AI

Upload both images into the AI tool below — the pattern serves as the style, and your artwork as the main image. Then print it.

4

Create another texture

Create another textured pattern with two more materials. Take a picture once you are done.

5

Repeat and print

Repeat step 3 with the new texture and the combined image, then print the result.

6

Exhibit, Share, and Reflect

Share your patterns and artwork with the group and use the discussion prompts to guide the conversation about ethics.
