AI Superhero

In this activity, you will create and think about unethical uses of AI to develop an AI superhero. You will use image-generative AI to build your superhero. Using AI and art materials, you will write your own hero’s journey and then edit it in comic book style. By turning these challenges into a story, you will engage with ethics principles. You can do this alone, with a partner, or in a small group.

What’s Needed

Also use other materials you can think of.

  • Comic panel examples
  • Image-generative AI
  • Art materials for character design

Guiding Questions for AI Ethics Literacy

Based on OECD AI principles.

Fairness and human values
  • Human values: Does your superhero have rules it will never break? What is the number one rule they follow to protect humans?
Transparency
  • Secret identity: Does the public in your comic know your hero is an AI, or do they think it is human? Is it okay to hide what it is?
Accountability
  • Responsibility: If your superhero breaks something while trying to save the day, who is responsible? The superhero, or the person who built them?

Activity Instructions

1

Imagine unethical scenarios

Brainstorm how AI could be used in unethical ways.

2

Design your hero

Using the materials and AI tools, design a superhero that advocates for AI ethics and can solve some of the scenarios imagined.

3

The hero’s journey

Develop a story for your hero. Shape it however you like!

4

Illustrate the journey

Use AI to illustrate the story you developed. Print or edit it into a comic strip page.

5

Exhibit and Share

Share your comic strip. What is your story, the ethical challenges, and your hero’s journey?

6

Reflect

Use the discussion prompts to guide the conversation about ethics.
